Reading a Kuwaiti car ad

Terms you will see in the listings

شرط الفحص

Sale is conditional on inspection. You can take the car for a check of the gearbox, engine and chassis (قير، مكينة، شاصي) before paying.

صبغ الوكالة

Original factory paint. The car has not been resprayed, which usually means no major body repairs.

وارد الساير / الوكالة

Sold new by the Kuwait agency (Al-Sayer for Toyota and Lexus, Al-Ghanim for Honda, Al-Mulla for Mitsubishi, Al-Babtain for Nissan).

ماشي ٢٠٠

Mileage is often written in thousands: "ماشي 200" means 200,000 km on the odometer.
